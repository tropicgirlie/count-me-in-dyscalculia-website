# Homepage Intro Narration — Count Me In

**Target length:** ~40–45 seconds at a calm pace
**Deliverable:** audio-only MP3, saved as `public/audio/homepage-intro.mp3`

## HeyGen production notes

- **Voice:** warm, calm, unhurried — a supportive friend, not a narrator at you. British English preferred (the site uses British spelling).
- **Pace:** 0.9–0.95× speed if the voice supports it. Leave breathing room at the dashes.
- **Export:** audio only, MP3, 128 kbps or higher.
- **File:** name it exactly `homepage-intro.mp3` and place it in `public/audio/`. The website detects the file automatically and shows the player only once it exists — nothing else needs to change.

## Script

> Welcome to Count Me In.
>
> If numbers have always felt harder for you than they seem to be for everyone else — if you still count on your fingers — if reading a clock takes a moment longer — if splitting a bill makes your mind go quiet — you are not broken. And you are not alone.
>
> Dyscalculia is a real, recognised difference in how the brain works with numbers. Around one in twenty people live with it — most without ever being told its name.
>
> Here, you'll find clear explanations, practical tools, and stories from people who understand.
>
> Take it at your own pace. Pause whenever you like.
>
> Numbers can move — and support can hold them still.
